SAF-T Financial

Frequently asked questions

Do you work from a SAF-T Financial file?

Yes — it is the first thing we ask for on a Norwegian target. SAF-T Financial carries transaction-level general ledger entries, so the monthly P&L, balance sheet and account detail all come from one structured export rather than a request the seller’s controller has to rebuild.

What changed with SAF-T v1.30?

Version 1.30 became the required format for accounting periods beginning 1 January 2025. It is an XML document structured as Header, Master Files and General Ledger Entries, and it is the version we read on current Norwegian engagements.

Which Norwegian ledger systems do you handle?

Tripletex, Visma, PowerOffice Go and Xledger cover most of the SME and mid-market segment. If the target runs something less common, send a sample export and we will confirm we can read it before you commit to anything.

How do you handle NGAAP versus IFRS?

We bridge reported NGAAP figures onto the basis you are underwriting and show the bridge rather than presenting a restated number without workings. Development costs, provisions and lease treatment are the divergences that most often move EBITDA.

Do you reconcile the statutory filing?

Yes. We reconcile the årsregnskap filed with Brønnøysundregistrene back to management reporting early in the engagement, because that reconciliation is reliably where a buyer’s first hard questions land.
