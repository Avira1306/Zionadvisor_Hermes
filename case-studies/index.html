Investor-grade databook for a SaaS founder

Minority investment process under a compressed timeline.

Challenge

  • Fragmented ledgers across entities
  • No ARR or net revenue retention visibility
  • Investor process already running

Approach

  • Standardised trial balances across entities
  • Mapped revenue streams to contract data
  • Built ARR, churn and profitability schedules

Impact

  • Diligence cycle compressed from four weeks to ten days
  • Audit-ready databook accepted by incoming investors

Bank statement and invoice reconciliation automation

Finance function losing 40–60 hours a month to manual matching.

Challenge

  • 40–60 hours per month spent reconciling statements
  • Error-prone manual matching
  • Month-end close slipping

Approach

  • Deployed AI parsing with auto-matching rules
  • Exception handling routed to the team channel
  • Documented the exception workflow

Impact

  • 50–70% of analyst time recovered
  • Month-end close accuracy improved

Frequently asked questions

Why are case studies anonymised?

Confidentiality is not a preference in our work; it is a condition of it. Our clients trust us with material non-public information, and we do not repay that trust by naming them without permission.

A mid-market buy-side or sell-side mandate, three to four weeks from data room to delivery, with a first-cut databook inside 72 hours. The output is an Excel databook with linked schedules, a reconciliation pack and a findings report.
