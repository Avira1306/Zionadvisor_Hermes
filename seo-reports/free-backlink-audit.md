## SECTION 7: WHAT'S MISSING / WHAT I CAN'T DO FOR FREE

| Capability | Free替代 | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Full backlink gap analysis (Ahrefs/Semrush) | Manual inspection of competitor sites | Can't see all links competitors have. Only finding public-facing "partners" pages. |
| Link quality scoring (DR/DA data) | Use free directory lists with published DA/DR | Can't score individual pages we find through search. |
| Automated link insertion ordering | insert.link was the tool — now excluded | Must do manual outreach or submissions. |
| Competitor link monitoring over time | Manual periodic checks | Can't track when competitors gain/lose links. |
| Indexation tracking for new links | GSC (partial) | GSC shows our links to us, not competitor links. |

**The gap is real**: without a paid tool, backlink building is slower and less data-driven. But the free methods above still produce real links — directories, guest posts, and outreach are how SEO worked before Ahrefs existed.
